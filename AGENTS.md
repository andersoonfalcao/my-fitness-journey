## Architecture
- Accounts: Firebase Auth (e-mail/password + Google) behind `AuthGate`; access is invite-only (`invites/{email}`, managed by the admin in Configurações, the gear in the header). Everyone confirms the e-mail (Google sign-in does it by itself; a password account gets the confirmation link). Server-side enforcement is `firestore.rules`, which also accepts only `{ json, updatedAt }` documents under `users/{uid}/data`; the Worker (`strava-worker`) applies the same invite check before answering.
- User data (profile, food log, weights, plan, sessions, history) lives in Firestore at `users/{uid}/data/{key}` as a JSON string, through `useLocal` in `src/lib/store.ts` (name kept from the localStorage era). Food logs are one document per month (`forma.foods.YYYY-MM`). Pre-account localStorage data is migrated once, to the first account signed in on the device.
- Built as a static SPA (`spa.enabled`, `nitro: false` in `vite.config.ts`) and hosted on Firebase Hosting's free plan: `dist/client`, every route rewritten to `/_shell.html` (`firebase.json`). Every push to `main` is published by `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` (secret `FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT`).
- Workout plans come from a method catalog (`src/lib/methods.ts`: full body, superior/inferior, PPL, divisão, força 5×5, circuito); `recommendMethod()` picks one from goal, level and days and the person can override it (`profile.method`). Plans are deterministic: variation 0 uses the original 24 exercises, each "trocar os exercícios" (`plan.variation`) draws from the whole library. Library: 192 exercises in 12 groups (panturrilha, trapézio, antebraço and condicionamento added 2026-10-05; 49 upper-body gym exercises added 2026-11-01; conditioning is done for time and left out of weekly sets); generation skips library entries without photos. Swaps stay within the same muscle group and only offer the same or a similar movement pattern.
- New plans go through "Novo treino" (`RegenerateDialog`): the person sets days, level and time, then either picks a goal (`planOptions()` in `methods.ts`: the methods that suit it, up to four) or writes one for the AI (`suggestPlans()` in `src/lib/ai-plan.ts`, three variations from the exercise library, `plan.ai`/`plan.goal`). Nothing changes until one is approved; saving the profile no longer replaces the plan, it opens this dialog when a training field changed.
- Finished workouts are one document per month (`forma.history.YYYY-MM`, months listed in `forma.history.months`) through `useHistory()`; the old single `forma.history` document is an inbox that `useHistory` empties into the months. Rejected cloud writes show a toast (`reportSaveError` in `cloud-store.ts`).
- Offline: `bun run build` also writes `dist/client/sw.js` from `scripts/sw-template.js` (precaches this build's JS/CSS; images are cached on first view). Registered only in production (`src/lib/offline.ts`).
- Load suggestions follow `loadAdvice()` in `src/lib/workout-stats.ts` (all target reps last time → +2.5 kg, +5 kg on compound leg lifts; missed twice → −10%).
- Exercise demos are start/end photos from free-exercise-db (public domain) in `src/assets/fedb` (WebP, 640 px wide), alternated with an accessible pause/play control; training load and set history are keyed by exercise ID and stay independent of regenerated plans.
- Bioimpedance: a photo or PDF of the report is read by the AI into the fields (`readBioimpedance()` in `src/lib/ai-body.ts`) and every saved reading gets an analysis (`analyzeBioimpedance()`, kept in `BodyEntry.analysis`). A reading can be approved for the daily goals (`profile.composition`, `CompositionTargets`): `targets()` then uses Katch-McArdle for the basal energy and protein per kg of lean mass.
- Food library is the TACO table (generated `src/lib/taco-data.ts`) plus a few USDA/label supplements; daily targets in `targets()` cite the studies they follow.
- Fechar o dia (`DayClose`) works for any date: Hoje shows today's; the Calendário's day dialog (`?dia=YYYY-MM-DD`) shows that day's checklist and closes it later. "Preencher" opens Alimentação/Corpo on that day through `?dia=` (`searchDay()` in `store.ts`); workouts can't be backdated. `closeDay()` only reads data up to the day it closes.
- Readings of the person's logs live in `src/lib/insights.ts` (pure functions, shared by the screens and the AI prompts): weight trend, pace, plateau and goal date, protein per meal, fibre (kept per food entry since 2026-10-12), weekday vs weekend calories, sleep regularity, cardio minutes, weeks on plan and signs of accumulated fatigue. `profile.maintain` holds the calories at maintenance once the goal weight is reached.
- Avisos (`/avisos`, the bell in the tab bar with an amber count): every notice of the app (notifications off, weight/food readings, calibration, measurements, fatigue, new medals) is listed by `NoticeList` in `src/components/NoticeCenter.tsx`; screens show none. A hidden copy in `AppShell` counts them for the badge.
- "Pergunte ao forma" (the sparkles button in the header, `src/components/CoachChat.tsx`) is a chat that answers about the person's own records: `coachData()` in `src/lib/coach.ts` builds the numbers (strength per exercise, last 12 workouts, 14 days of food, weight, weeks, sleep) and they go with every question; the conversation stays on the device (`forma.coach` in localStorage).
- Updates: `src/lib/offline.ts` checks for a new service worker whenever the app comes back to the screen and reloads once when one takes over; Configurações shows the version (the `VERSION` of `sw.js`). The refresh button next to the calendar in the header (`RefreshButton` in `AppShell.tsx`) is data only: it first runs the "forma Saúde" Shortcut on an iPhone that already sent Health data this month (`runHealthShortcut()` in `health.ts`, waits for the return to the app), then syncs Strava; app updates stay automatic and in Configurações.
- AI food estimation calls Gemini from the browser through Firebase AI Logic (`src/lib/ai-food.ts`, free tier). App Check becomes mandatory for AI Logic on 2026-11-02.
- Design system (2026-11-18): every colour, type size, radius, shadow and duration is a token in `src/styles.css` (foundation tokens per theme in `:root`/`.dark`, the shadcn names pointed at them, Tailwind classes such as `bg-surface-2`, `text-warning-text`, `bg-brand-soft`). No hex colours in components: the only exceptions are the Strava orange (`--brand-strava`), the Google logo and the share image palette (`SHARE_COLORS` in `share-card.ts`, a copy of the dark tokens because it is a picture). Emerald (`accent`/`primary`) is for main actions, progress and goals met; amber (`warning`) only for alerts and the streak. Light-theme text and filled buttons use `--accent-strong` (#047857) for AA contrast. Shared pieces: `Button` (with `loading`), `Card`/`Eyebrow`/`Callout` (`ui/card.tsx`), `Badge`, `ProgressBar`/`ProgressRing` (`ui/meter.tsx`), `EmptyState`, `Skeleton`/`ScreenSkeleton`, the `surface-card`, `type-*`, `pressable` and `animate-pop` utilities. Theme: follows the device unless a choice is saved (`forma.theme`, Sistema/Claro/Escuro in Configurações, `src/lib/theme.ts`).
- Navigation (2026-11-18): tab bar (below 1024 px) is Hoje, Treinos, "+", Evolução, Avisos; the "+" opens `QuickLog` (refeição, peso, água, cardio, medidas, suplemento, and links to Alimentação and Corpo); Perfil is the avatar in the header; wide screens get the full top menu (`MENU` in `AppShell.tsx`).
- Hoje: readiness 0–100 (`ReadinessCard` in `HealthToday.tsx`, `readinessScore()` in `health.ts`, built on `readiness()` so the figure never contradicts the text; only with Mi Band data), then the day's rings (`ActivityRings` in `ui/meter.tsx`: calories, protein, water) and the workout card.
- Treinos: finishing a workout opens `WorkoutComplete` (time, sets, volume, records, comparison, "como foi?", share) instead of toasts; focus mode (`focusPref`, `forma.focus` in localStorage) shows the open exercise card full screen and dark (`.focus-overlay`), with prev/next, swipe and the sets circle centred.
- Alimentação: macro donut, meals as a timeline with a "+" each; adding food happens in a bottom sheet (the `#registrar` hash opens it) with Foto do prato / Adicionar fixed above the tab bar.
- Evolução (`/evolucao`): 12-week constancy map (a point each for training, food logged and day closed), weight trend with the goal, strength per exercise. Fotos (`/fotos`): progress photos per pose, each a JPEG data URL shrunk to 900 px in its own document `forma.photo.{id}` (list in `forma.photos`), no Cloud Storage; `PhotosNotice` reminds two weeks after the last one.
- Spotify (2026-11-20): a remote control for the person's Spotify during the workout (`src/lib/spotify.ts`, Spotify Connect: the music plays in the Spotify app). Login is PKCE from the browser, back at `/spotify`; tokens in `forma.spotify` (refresh rotated on use). Client ID: `SPOTIFY_CLIENT_ID`, else the one pasted in Configurações (`forma.spotify.app`). `SpotifyBar` (`SpotifyPlayer.tsx`) sits in the workout's sticky bar and in focus mode's top bar: cover, song, prev/play/next, and a sheet with the person's playlists and a search (playlists, albums). Controlling needs Premium; in development mode every account must be in the Spotify app's User Management.
